Join NEU FC

Choose the membership tier that fits you best. All plans are designed for Near East University students who want to stay active, improve their football skills, and be part of a competitive campus community.

Why Join?

Membership gives students a structured path to improve fitness, learn tactical systems, and represent NEU in university competitions. Contact us through the form on our Contact page to request a trial session.
