Career

Hamilton debuted with McLaren in 2007 and won his first championship in 2008. He moved to Mercedes in 2013 and dominated the hybrid era, equalling Michael Schumacher's record of seven world titles.

In 2025 he joined Ferrari — one of the most talked-about moves in recent F1 history. Silverstone remains his home race, where British fans turn the grandstands red.

Legacy

He holds the record for most race wins and pole positions in Formula 1. Beyond the track he has pushed for diversity in motorsport and spoken publicly about environmental issues.
