Our Facilities

Campus Athletic Infrastructure

  • Main Stadium: A natural grass pitch equipped with floodlights for evening matches and official university tournaments.
  • Indoor Arena: Artificial turf center for extreme weather conditions and focused tactical drills.
  • Performance Gym: A fully equipped weight room for strength, conditioning, and injury prevention programs.

Essential Equipment

Players are encouraged to use proper gear for safety and performance. Football boots should match the pitch surface, shin guards are required in all training sessions, and goalkeepers use specialized gloves for shot stopping.

Match Day Setup

Training cones, bibs, rebounders, and match balls are provided by the club. Our facilities support both competitive match preparation and beginner-friendly development sessions for new members.
