Our Facilities

Campus Facilities

  • Main Pitch: Grass field for matches and training.
  • Indoor Hall: Used for training in bad weather.
  • Gym: Basic equipment for strength and fitness.

Equipment

Players should wear proper football boots, shin guards, and comfortable sportswear. The club provides balls, cones, and training bibs during sessions.
