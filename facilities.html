Stadium & Training Grounds

Our players deserve the best environments to hone their skills.

  • Main Stadium: A 5,000-capacity natural grass pitch equipped with floodlights for evening matches.
  • Indoor Training Center: Artificial turf arenas for extreme weather conditions and tactical drills.
  • Performance Lab: Equipped with state-of-the-art diagnostic tools to track player speed, stamina, and physical development.
