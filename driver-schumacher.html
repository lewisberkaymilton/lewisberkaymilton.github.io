Career

Schumacher won two titles with Benetton (1994, 1995), then moved to Ferrari and transformed the team into a dominant force. He won five consecutive championships from 2000 to 2004.

He retired in 2006, returned with Mercedes in 2010, and retired permanently in 2012. His work ethic and fitness level set new standards for how F1 drivers prepare.

Ferrari Era

The Schumacher-Ferrari partnership is one of the most successful in F1. Together they ended Ferrari's long championship drought and built a culture of discipline inside the team.
