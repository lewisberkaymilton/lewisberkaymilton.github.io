Career

Max Verstappen made his F1 debut with Toro Rosso at age 17 — the youngest starter in championship history. He won his first race with Red Bull at the 2016 Spanish Grand Prix on his debut for the team.

With Red Bull he won multiple world championships in the early 2020s, often fighting wheel-to-wheel battles and showing strong race craft in wet conditions. His aggressive overtakes and defensive driving are a major part of his reputation.

Driving Style

Verstappen is known for late braking, strong tyre management, and confidence in changing conditions. He works closely with race engineers on strategy calls during grands prix.
