Iconic Circuits

Every track has a different personality — straights, corners, and weather included.

Tracks On The Calendar

The calendar changes every year, but a few circuits always stand out. Some reward top speed; others punish mistakes with walls close to the asphalt.

  • Monza (Italy) — The "Temple of Speed". Long straights, low downforce setups, huge slipstream battles.
  • Monaco — Narrow streets, zero margin for error. Qualifying position is basically everything.
  • Silverstone (UK) — Fast corners, changeable weather, home race energy for British teams.
  • Spa-Francorchamps (Belgium) — Eau Rouge is the famous corner. Often rainy, always dramatic.
  • Suzuka (Japan) — Figure-of-eight layout. Loved by drivers for flow and technical challenge.
  • Interlagos (Brazil) — Altitude, passionate fans, and late-season championship memories.

Street vs Permanent Tracks

Street circuits (Monaco, Singapore, Las Vegas) are bumpy and tight. Permanent tracks give teams more room to experiment with car setup. That is why you see completely different qualifying results when the calendar moves from one type to the other.
