Part 2: Conclusions and Web Statistics

Conclusion: Based on the survey conducted in my local circle, e-commerce is deeply embedded in the habits of the 18–25 age group. A vast majority of these young adults shop online monthly or weekly, heavily prioritizing convenience and speed. While local platforms like Trendyol are popular, global giants like Amazon hold the largest share among my respondents.

Supporting Statistics: These localized results align perfectly with global digital market trends. Research from the Pew Internet & American Life Project shows that approximately 90% of young adults (18-29) are active online shoppers. Furthermore, reports from eMarketer confirm that convenience, diverse product options, and mobile accessibility are the primary drivers of frequent online purchases for Millennials and Gen Z across the globe.
