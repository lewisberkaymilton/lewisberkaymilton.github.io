E-Commerce Popularity Survey

A survey conducted among 20 individuals (friends, family, and classmates) to determine the popularity and habits of online shopping.

1. Survey Findings

  • a. Purchased an item online: 18 people (90%)
  • b. Shopped but not purchased online: 2 people (10%)
  • c. Purchase frequency: Once a week (5 people), Once a month (10 people), Once a year (3 people)
  • d. Age range: 18–25 (14 people), 26–39 (4 people), 40–50 (2 people), Over 50 (0 people)
  • e. Gender: 12 Male, 8 Female
  • f. Level of education: High school (2), Some college (14), College graduate (3), Graduate school (1)
  • g. Favorite online shopping site: Amazon (10), Trendyol (6), AliExpress (2)

2. Conclusions & Market Research

Based on the survey of 20 participants, it is evident that e-commerce is deeply integrated into daily life, particularly among the 18–25 demographic (predominantly "some college" students). A significant 90% of respondents have made online purchases, with a majority shopping on a monthly or weekly basis. Amazon and local giants like Trendyol dominate as the preferred platforms. The minimal percentage (10%) of users who only "window shop" suggests that consumer trust in digital payment systems is currently very high.

These local findings are heavily supported by broader industry statistics. According to recent reports by eMarketer, global retail e-commerce sales continue to climb, driven by digital-native generations (Gen Z and Millennials) who prioritize convenience. Furthermore, data from the Pew Research Center highlights that the vast majority of young adults are online almost constantly, making them the most likely demographic to engage in frequent digital commerce. The shift from physical retail to e-commerce is no longer a trend, but a permanent consumer standard.
