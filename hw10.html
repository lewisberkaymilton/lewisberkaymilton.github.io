Conclusions and Web Statistics

Conclusion: Based on my local survey results, e-commerce is highly integrated into the daily lives of the 18–25 demographic (mostly "some college" students). A significant 90% of respondents have made online purchases. These local findings are strongly supported by global statistics. According to recent reports by eMarketer, retail e-commerce sales continue to grow rapidly, driven by young adults who prioritize convenience. Furthermore, data from the Pew Internet and American Life Project highlights that the vast majority of this age group is online almost constantly, making them the primary drivers of platforms like Amazon and Trendyol. The shift to digital commerce is clearly a permanent standard.
