Why It Matters

Monza is called the Temple of Speed. Long straights and fast chicanes mean teams run very low downforce wings. Slipstreaming battles into Turn 1 are common, and races are often decided by DRS trains on the main straight.

Famous Corners

Parabolica — long high-speed right-hander onto the straight. Ascari chicane — heavy braking after the back straight. Lesmo curves — technical middle sector that tests car balance.
