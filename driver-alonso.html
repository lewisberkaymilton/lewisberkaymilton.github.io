Career

Alonso won back-to-back championships with Renault in 2005 and 2006, beating Schumacher's Ferrari at the time. He later drove for McLaren, Ferrari, and Alpine before joining Aston Martin.

He also won the Le Mans 24 Hours twice and competed in the Indianapolis 500 — rare achievements for a modern F1 driver.

Longevity

Still racing at the highest level well into his 40s, Alonso is known for sharp race starts, defensive driving, and detailed radio feedback to engineers.
