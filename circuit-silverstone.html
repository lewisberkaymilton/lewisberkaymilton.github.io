Why It Matters

Silverstone hosted the very first Formula 1 world championship race in 1950. It is a high-speed circuit with fast corners like Copse and Maggots/Becketts. British fans create one of the loudest atmospheres on the calendar.

Weather

English summer weather is unpredictable. Rain during the race can shuffle the order completely, which makes Silverstone weekends exciting even when one team looks dominant in dry practice.
