Teams & Constructors

F1 is won in the factory as much as on track.

2025 Grid Snapshot

Line-ups can change between seasons, but the big teams stay familiar. Budget, wind tunnel time, and staff size still decide who fights at the front.

Team Base Notes
Red Bull Racing UK / Austria Strong aero department; dominated recent hybrid-era seasons.
Ferrari Italy Oldest team in F1. Huge fan base, always under pressure to win.
Mercedes UK / Germany Hybrid-era powerhouse; now rebuilding for new rules.
McLaren UK Historic team back near the front after tough years.
Aston Martin UK Investing heavily in factory and facilities.
Williams UK Independent team with deep history, working back up the grid.

Constructors' Championship

Points from both drivers count toward the constructors' title. That is why teams care about having two consistent scorers — one star driver is not enough if the second car finishes outside the points every week.

Driver line-ups and results change every season. I update this page based on what I follow during the year.
