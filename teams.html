Constructors' Championship

Each team runs two cars. The combined points decide the constructors' title — which brings more prize money and prestige. A team with one superstar and one slow second driver rarely wins the championship.

Power Unit Suppliers

Only four engine manufacturers supply the grid in 2025: Mercedes (McLaren, Williams, Aston Martin), Ferrari (Ferrari, Haas, Sauber), Renault (Alpine), and Honda RBPT (Red Bull, RB). Engine rules change again in 2026.
