Formula 1 — Speed, Strategy, Pressure

A small fan site about the sport I watch every race weekend.

Why I Follow F1

I did not grow up around race tracks. I started watching Formula 1 because a friend kept talking about qualifying and tyre strategy. After a few races, I was hooked — the starts are chaos, the pit stops are timed to the second, and one small mistake can ruin a whole weekend.

This project is my final homework site. It is not an official F1 page — just my notes on the history, drivers, tracks, and teams that make the sport interesting.

What Makes F1 Different

It is not only about who has the fastest car. Teams spend millions on aerodynamics, engineers study data lap by lap, and drivers have to stay focused for two hours in crazy heat. That mix of machine and human skill is what keeps me watching.
