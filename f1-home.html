A Journey Through Formula 1

Formula 1 is the highest class of international racing. Ten teams, twenty drivers, and 24 race weekends per season. This site covers the history of the sport, six legendary drivers, six famous circuits, and the full current grid.

What Is Formula 1?

F1 cars are open-wheel, single-seat machines built to strict technical rules. Each team designs its own chassis and works with a power-unit supplier (Mercedes, Ferrari, Renault, or Honda RBPT). Races are held on permanent tracks and street circuits across five continents.

A race weekend has three practice sessions, qualifying, and a Sunday grand prix. Points are awarded to the top ten finishers. The driver and constructor with the most points at the end of the season win the world championships.
