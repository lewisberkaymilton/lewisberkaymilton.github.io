Career

Senna won three championships with McLaren (1988, 1990, 1991). His rivalry with Alain Prost defined the late 1980s. He was especially famous for stunning qualifying laps and exceptional speed in wet weather.

His 1994 move to Williams ended tragically at the San Marino Grand Prix at Imola. Senna remains a national hero in Brazil and one of the most respected names in motorsport history.

Monaco Master

Senna won the Monaco Grand Prix six times — more than any other driver until the record was broken decades later. The narrow street circuit suited his precise, aggressive style.
