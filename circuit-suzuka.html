Why It Matters

Suzuka is one of few figure-eight layouts in F1. The Esses, Spoon Curve, and 130R demand perfect rhythm. Japanese fans are passionate — the grandstands are often full for the whole weekend.

Championship Deciders

Several world championships have been decided at Suzuka, including famous Senna-Prost collisions in 1989 and 1990.
