Welcome to NEU FC: The Beautiful Game ⚽

Welcome to Near East University Football Club. We bring students together who love football and want to play at a good level. This website shares basic information about our club, training, facilities, and membership.

What is Football?

Football is a team sport played with a ball on a field. Two teams try to score goals by getting the ball into the opponent's net. It requires running, passing, teamwork, and discipline.

Players must work together, follow the coach's plan, and stay fit throughout the season. Football is not only about skill, but also about respect and fair play.

Our Core Values

  • Teamwork
  • Discipline
  • Respect
  • Hard Work
