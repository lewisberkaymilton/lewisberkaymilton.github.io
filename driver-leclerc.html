Career

Leclerc won the F2 championship in 2017 and joined Sauber in 2018. He moved to Ferrari in 2019 as a clear future star. He has taken multiple pole positions at tracks like Baku and Monaco.

In 2025 he shares Ferrari with Lewis Hamilton — pairing two generations of Ferrari drivers on the same team.

Qualifying Speed

Leclerc is often among the fastest drivers over a single lap. His main challenge has been converting poles into race wins when the car is not the fastest on Sundays.
