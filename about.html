About The Club

Who We Are

NEU FC is the football club of Near East University. We welcome students who want to train, play matches, and represent the university in local tournaments.

Coaching Staff

Our coaches help players improve their basic skills such as passing, shooting, and defending. They also focus on fitness and teamwork during every session.

Club Activities

We organize weekly training, friendly matches, and campus events. Students can join to stay active and meet other football lovers at NEU.
