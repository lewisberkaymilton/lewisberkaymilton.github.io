Training & Nutrition

Training Focus

Our training sessions include warm-up, ball control drills, passing exercises, and small-sided games. Players also do fitness work to build speed and endurance.

Weekly Schedule

  • Monday: Passing and ball control
  • Tuesday: Fitness and running drills
  • Wednesday: Rest day
  • Thursday: Tactical training
  • Friday: Match practice

Nutrition

Players are advised to eat balanced meals, drink enough water, and get enough protein for recovery. Good nutrition helps performance on match day.
