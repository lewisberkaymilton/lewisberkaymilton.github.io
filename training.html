Training & Nutrition

The Core Pillar: Consistent Practice

Balancing academic life with football requires smart, focused training. Our players combine technical drills, physical conditioning, and recovery to perform at their best when match day arrives.

Technical Drills

Ball control, first touch, passing accuracy, and shooting technique form the foundation of every good player. Short passing drills, cone dribbling, and finishing exercises build confidence in high-pressure match situations.

Physical Conditioning

Football demands speed, agility, and endurance. Sprint intervals, shuttle runs, and core strength work prepare players for the constant movement required over 90 minutes. Recovery sessions and stretching reduce injury risk throughout the season.

Weekly Schedule

Our players follow a structured training plan combining pitch work and gym sessions.

Day Training Focus
Monday Tactical Drills & Lower Body Gym
Tuesday Pressing, Possession & Core Stability
Wednesday Rest & Video Analysis
Thursday Set Pieces & Upper Body Gym
Friday Match Simulation & Agility
Weekend Match Day or Active Recovery Walk

Nutrition Plan

To support this intense routine and maintain performance, players receive guidance on balanced meal planning. Protein intake is prioritized for muscle recovery, while carbohydrates fuel training sessions and matches. Hydration before, during, and after exercise is essential for every athlete at NEU FC.
