Training & Nutrition

Weekly Training & Nutrition Plan

Technical ability means nothing without physical endurance. Our players follow a strict 5-day-a-week gym schedule alongside pitch sessions to ensure peak performance.

Physical Conditioning Protocol

To maintain muscle mass during intense cardio sessions, all NEU FC athletes are required to follow a high-protein diet. Meal portions are specifically increased to meet a strict threshold of at least 130 grams of protein daily. This ensures rapid recovery and sustained explosive power on the pitch.

Day Pitch Session Gym Focus
Monday Tactical Positioning Lower Body Power
Tuesday High-Intensity Pressing Core & Stability
Wednesday Rest / Video Analysis Active Recovery
Thursday Set Pieces & Finishing Upper Body Strength
Friday Full Match Simulation Agility & Speed
