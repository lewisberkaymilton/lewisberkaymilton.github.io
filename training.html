Training & Nutrition

Weekly Schedule

Our players follow a strict 5-day training schedule combining pitch work and heavy gym sessions.

Nutrition Plan

To support this intense routine and build muscle mass, players are given customized diet plans. Meal portions are specifically increased to ensure a minimum daily intake of 130 grams of protein for optimal recovery.

Day Training Focus
Monday Tactical Drills & Lower Body Gym
Tuesday Pressing & Core Stability
Wednesday Rest & Video Analysis
Thursday Set Pieces & Upper Body Gym
Friday Match Simulation & Agility
