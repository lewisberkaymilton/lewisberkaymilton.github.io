Why It Matters

Spa is the longest circuit on the calendar. Eau Rouge / Raidillon is the most famous sequence — flat-out for modern F1 cars, but terrifying in the rain. The Ardennes forest weather can change lap by lap.

Driver Favourite

Many drivers list Spa as their favourite track because it rewards bravery and car balance. Overtaking is possible at Bus Stop chicane and Les Combes.
